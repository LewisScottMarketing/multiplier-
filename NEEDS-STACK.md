Customer sketch (unvetted — no CAROL.md yet): a school or Multi-Academy
Trust (MAT) in England, acting through school HR, the school business
manager, or occasionally the head teacher, who must run safer recruitment
on every hire and keep the Single Central Record (SCR) accurate for DBS
checks and inspections. Product: a KCSiE compliance tool handling job
application submissions and SCR record-keeping. Levels are numbered in
settle order; position in the list below carries the stack order, top
ends before bottom means.

    Role: **what you do** — features live here; meet searchers where
    they are.  ← YOU ARE HERE
