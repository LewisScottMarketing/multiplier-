Customer sketch (unvetted — no CAROL.md yet): a school or Multi-Academy
Trust (MAT) in England, acting through school HR, the school business
manager, or occasionally the head teacher, who must run safer recruitment
on every hire and keep the Single Central Record (SCR) accurate for DBS
checks and inspections. Product: a KCSiE compliance tool handling job
application submissions and SCR record-keeping. Levels are numbered in
settle order; position in the list below carries the stack order, top
ends before bottom means.

## The stack

**N5.** ★ Do the job I went into education for, knowing I did everything I needed to keep children safe, so I can switch off at the end of the day without dreading an inspection or an incident. — *purpose level: no product satisfies this directly; material for higher-purpose stories, never for promises.*

**N4.** → Run a school where no child is harmed by an adult we hired, and parents know it.
    Occupants: safeguarding training providers; the school's designated safeguarding lead; concern-recording systems such as CPOMS. (Unverified — confirm with the user.)
    Role: aspiration — show the part you play; never promise it.
    Why your level still wins for your ideal customer: those cover what happens once staff are in the building; the tool covers who gets in and whether the record of it is right.

**N3.** → Be fully compliant with KCSiE on staff vetting, so Ofsted finds nothing wrong when it checks.
    Occupants: local authority safeguarding audit and support teams; external safeguarding auditors and consultants; a trust's own central compliance team.
    Role: **the promise** — the outcome claimed as a consequence of N1. KCSiE is a mandatory duty, so this is a must-have, not an aspiration.
    Why your level still wins for your ideal customer: audits and consultants check at a point in time and report gaps; the tool keeps the records right every day, so there is little for an audit to find. (Draft — confirm with the user.)

**N1.** → Know, and be able to prove instantly to an inspector, that every member of staff was safely recruited and is still properly vetted.
    Occupants: you; CPOMS (StaffSafe module); School SCR / MAT SCR; Online SCR; The Access Group (HR suite with SCR module).
    Role: **what you do** — features live here; meet searchers where
    they are.  ← YOU ARE HERE

**N2.** → Track each staff member's vetting checks in a spreadsheet, and chase references and documents by hand for every hire.
    Occupants: Excel and Google Sheets; email and phone for references; shared drives (e.g. SharePoint); the DBS Update Service portal.
    Role: advancement — a step you make obsolete; brag about it.
